What this is

Research Feed is a scrollable feed of short stories about new research. Reading is free. No login. Vizmaya publishes every story and is responsible for it.

Money comes from an optional Pro plan ($9 a month or $79 a year) and from stories that are clearly labeled Paid Advertisement. We never paywall the research itself.

How the score works

The score runs from 0 to 100. It is an automated opinion. Research signal. Not investment advice.

  • Only outside signals count: citations per month against the field average, patent citations, code or data released, industry co-authors, funder type, and readiness cues found in the paper.
  • Rules are fixed and versioned. A model reads the paper. The rules do the scoring.
  • Anything an author can edit is never used. Nobody can pay to change a score or a place in the feed. Sponsored stories use the same rules.
  • Very new papers show "Too new to score", with a confidence level. We never show a fake zero.
  • We score the work, never the person.

Think a score is wrong? Press "Dispute this score" on the story. A human reviews it within 48 hours.

How we use papers

Before we write anything we read the paper's license on the exact version. Open licenses (CC BY, CC0) allow credit-based reuse. Everything else is ideas and facts only: our own words, our own charts, no copied figures. If we cannot find a license, we do not publish.

Every number has a "Source" line. Numbers we calculate are marked "computed" with the formula. Every story ends with a credit block. Stories are not endorsed by the authors or their institutions.

AI labels

Every story and score carries an "AI-assisted" label. The page also carries machine-readable marking in its metadata. A second automated step re-checks every number. A human editor approves each story.

Privacy

  • Readers need no account. We do not track you across sites.
  • Saves, streaks and audio limits for free readers live in your browser only.
  • Pro needs your name and email, and you must be 18 or older. Payments go through a merchant of record, so we never see your card.
  • Delete your account inside the app (Pro panel). Personal data is erased. Billing consent records are kept as the law requires, with your email hashed.
  • We do not scrape or profile people who did not join.

Every story has "Report an error" and "I am an author: remove this story" buttons at the end. An error report puts the story in "Under review" at once, and we fix confirmed errors within 24 hours. A removal request takes the story down first. Questions come after.
