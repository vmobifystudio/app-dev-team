# Creator terms (draft, phase 3, behind a flag)

> **Not legal advice.** Draft for lawyer review. Creator onboarding is not built in the MVP.

**Plain version: tell the story of your own work. Do not make an offer. Do not claim what you cannot show.**

## 1. Who can submit
A researcher or founder describing their own work. We verify you by ORCID sign-in or an institutional email that matches the paper's affiliation.

## 2. Required tags
You must say, truthfully: "I am the author", "I am the founder", "I hold equity". A story missing any tag cannot be submitted.

## 3. Nothing here is an offer
This site hosts research. It never hosts offers.
- Do not post a round size, a valuation, a term, or the words "invest" or "join our round".
- Do not ask for money or promise a return.
- Link only to your own public site.
- We do not match you with investors and we do not collect investor details.
A post that breaks this is removed, and we may close the account.

## 4. No fake proof
No made-up testimonials. No return promises. No claims about traction you cannot show with a public source.

## 5. Dangerous detail
No step-by-step detail that would help someone build a weapon or a biological threat. Stories on sensitive topics go to a person for review.

## 6. Your rights and ours
- You confirm: "I am an author or I have the rights."
- We use the same license gate as for our own stories. Your story is checked against the paper's license.
- We publish under Vizmaya's name. We may edit for clarity, accuracy and these rules.
- You can reply to your story's score. You can dispute it. A person reviews within 48 hours.

## 7. Copyright complaints
Send complaints to our designated agent: *[name, address, email after registration]*. We respond within 24 hours (assumption). We follow the US notice and counter-notice process. India: *[grievance officer]*.

## 8. Free
Creator submission is free. We do not sell placement. Paid stories are a separate product and are labeled "Paid Advertisement".
