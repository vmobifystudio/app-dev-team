# Content rules (draft), including the sponsored label

> **Not legal advice.** Draft for lawyer review.

**Plain version: we publish ideas and facts, in our own words, with a source for every number.**

## 1. License gate (runs before anything is drafted)
Read the license on the paper's page for the exact version. Versions can differ.

| License | What we may do |
|---|---|
| CC BY, CC0 | Reuse with credit. House style: our own words, short credited quotes only. |
| CC BY-SA | Ideas only. Share-alike clashes with a paid layer. |
| CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-ND, CC BY-NC-SA, CC BY-ND, arXiv default, publisher copyright | Ideas and facts only. Own words, own charts, own structure. No copied sentences. No copied or traced figures. No section-by-section mirroring. |
| Unknown | Do not publish. |

- We never reproduce a paper's figure or table design. Charts are rebuilt from the numbers.
- A copy check blocks any 6-word run shared with the paper (outside declared CC BY quotes).
- Photos: CC BY, CC0 or public domain by default. CC BY-SA only if unedited. Always show author, source link and license.
- We keep a log of paper, version, license, date checked, and the source of every figure, photo and quote.

## 2. Every story ends with a credit block
"Source paper: [title]. [authors], [institution]. arXiv:[id], [date]. [Preprint, not peer reviewed.] Paper license: [license]. This story reuses none of its text or figures. [Photo credits.] Not affiliated with or endorsed by the authors or [institution]. [AI-assisted label.]"
When a CC BY story reuses a short quote, the block says so instead of "reuses none".

## 3. Numbers and claims
- One point per card. Plain English a 12-year-old follows.
- Every number has a "Source" line. Numbers we calculate say "computed" and show the formula.
- A claim with no source is cut.
- A "Contested" box appears when a headline claim and the paper's own tables disagree.
- A preprint warning appears when the paper is not peer reviewed.
- Every image has alt text. Every audio or animation has captions or a text description.

## 4. Never in any story
- Round size, valuation, "invest", "join our round", terms of any offer.
- Return promises, fake testimonials, investor matching.
- Step-by-step detail that would help build a weapon or a biological threat.
- Claims about a person. We score the work, never the person.

## 5. Score rules
Outside signals only. Rules are public and versioned. Label: "Automated opinion. Research signal. Not investment advice." Very new papers show "Too new to score", never a fake 0. No payment changes a score or a place in the feed.

## 6. AI labels
Every AI-assisted story and score shows "AI-assisted". Pages also carry machine-readable marking.

## 7. Sponsored stories (phase 3)
- The label is **"Paid Advertisement"** (or "Ad"), shown **inside every card**, so it travels when a card is shared.
- Not allowed as the only label: "Sponsored by", "Presented by".
- Same license gate, same checker, same score rules, same credit block. Sponsors get no say in the score.
- The credit block adds: "Paid Advertisement: Vizmaya was paid to make this story."
- Price assumption: $500 per story.

## 8. Corrections and removals
- Reported error: story shows "Under review" at once. Fix within 24 hours (assumption).
- Author says "remove": story goes down the same day, questions after.
- Withdrawn paper: story is pulled.
- Paper updated: story shows "Based on vN" until reviewed.
- Score dispute: reviewed within 48 hours (assumption).
