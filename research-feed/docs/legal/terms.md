# Terms (draft)

> **Not legal advice.** Draft for lawyer review.

**Plain version: reading is free. Pro is optional. Stories are research summaries, not advice.**

## 1. Who we are
Vizmaya publishes Research Feed. We write every story and we are responsible for it.

## 2. Reading
You do not need an account. You may read, share links and quote short parts with credit. You may not scrape the site at a rate that harms it.

## 3. What our stories are
Stories explain published research in plain English. They are AI-assisted and edited by a person. They are not advice of any kind. Scores are an automated opinion built from outside signals. They are not investment advice and not an offer or a request for money.

## 4. Pro
- Price and the next renewal date are shown above the pay button before you pay.
- Pro renews automatically only if you tick the renewal box yourself. The box starts unticked.
- Cancel in one click, in the same place you signed up. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
- Annual plans: we email you 7 days before renewal.
- Payments are handled by a merchant of record. They handle tax and your card. We never see your card.
- You must be 18 or older.
- Refunds: *[lawyer to set]*.

## 4a. What Pro gives you
Alerts for followed fields (about 1 hour after publishing, not guaranteed), saves synced across devices, filters, CSV export, audio for every story. We never put the research itself behind Pro.

## 5. Reports and takedowns
Every story has "Report an error" and an author removal button. Error reports show "Under review" at once. We aim to fix confirmed errors within 24 hours. An author's request to remove a story takes it down first. A person reviews it after.

## 6. Disputes about a score
Press "Dispute this score". A person reviews it within 48 hours.

## 7. Our limits
We do our best to be accurate. We cannot promise that every story is free of error. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses from relying on a story. *[lawyer to set the limit and the governing law]*

## 8. Changes
We will tell Pro users by email before we change price or renewal terms.
