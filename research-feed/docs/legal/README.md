# Documents for one lawyer review

> **Not legal advice.** These are plain-English drafts written by a product team and an AI model. They are not reviewed by a lawyer. Do not publish them as final. Send all four to one lawyer together so the terms, the creator terms, the privacy notice and the content rules agree with each other.

| File | Covers | Open questions for the lawyer |
|---|---|---|
| `terms.md` | Reader and Pro terms, billing, liability | Auto-renewal rules by US state and country. Wording of the liability limit. |
| `creator-terms.md` | Phase 3 creators. Securities and honesty rules. | Whether our "no offers" rule is enough. Whether we need a securities disclaimer by country. |
| `privacy-notice.md` | Data we hold, rights, India DPDP, EU | DPDP duties from May 13, 2027. Whether name and email only keeps us out of extra duties. |
| `content-rules.md` | Story rules, license policy, AI labels, sponsored label | Whether "ideas and facts only" is a safe reading of CC BY-NC-ND. Whether our own AI marking is enough. |

Before launch (human tasks, not code):
1. Register a DMCA designated agent with the US Copyright Office ($6, renew every 3 years).
2. Name the India grievance officer and the privacy contact. Fill the placeholders on `/about/`.
3. Confirm the merchant of record handles tax, card and renewal-notice rules for the countries you sell in.
