# Privacy notice (draft)

> **Not legal advice.** Draft for lawyer review.

**Plain version: readers are anonymous. Pro users give a name and an email. Nothing else.**

## What we hold

| Who | What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reader | Nothing about you. Saves, streaks and audio limits live in your browser. Our server logs normal request lines. | Run the site | Log retention: *[lawyer and ops to set, suggest 30 days]* |
| Pro user | Name, email, plan, renewal date, followed fields, saved lists | Run Pro | Until you delete your account |
| Pro consent | The exact words you agreed to, price, renewal date, time. Your email is stored only as a hash after you delete. | Prove consent for billing law | As the law requires *[lawyer to set]* |
| Payments | Held by the merchant of record, not us | Take payment | Their policy |
| Reporter | The email you give when you report a story | Reply to you | Until the report is closed |

We never scrape or profile people who did not join. We do not sell data. We do not run ad trackers.

## Your rights
- **Delete my account:** press it inside the app (Pro panel). Name, email, follows, saves and alerts are erased. Consent records stay with a hashed reference.
- **Cancel:** one click, in the Pro panel.
- **Ask a question or complain:** *[named grievance contact and email]*.
