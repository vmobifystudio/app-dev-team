# Email templates

> Not legal advice. Draft.

## 1. Author email (sent after publishing)

**Subject:** We wrote a short plain-English story about your paper

Hi [first author name],

We published a short story about your paper "[title]" (arXiv:[id]).
Here is the link: [story link]

What we reused: nothing. [For CC BY: one short quote, with credit.] Charts are rebuilt from your numbers. We do not copy figures.

Where our numbers differ from your headline:
[List each Contested box in one line, or "none".]

If you see an error, reply or use the "Report an error" button. We will fix it within 24 hours.
If you want the story removed, reply "remove". It comes down the same day.

The story says it is not affiliated with or endorsed by you.

Vizmaya

## 2. Renewal reminder (annual plans, 7 days before)

**Subject:** Your Research Feed Pro plan renews in 7 days

Hi [name],
Your annual plan renews on [date] at $79.00.
To stop it, open Pro in the app and press Cancel. One click.

## 3. Field alert (Pro)

**Subject:** New in [field]: [title]

A new story in [field] just went live: [title] [link]
You get this because you follow [field]. Unfollow any time in the app.

## 4. Takedown acknowledgement

**Subject:** Your story is down

We took "[title]" down the moment we got your note. A person will follow up today. If you want it back or changed, tell us what you need.
